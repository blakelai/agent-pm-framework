# Effort and delivery forecasting

Apply [Evidence and Clarification Policy](../Evidence-and-Clarification-Policy.md) whenever information is unclear, missing or conflicting. Ask the user for clarification or relevant material; keep dependent conclusions pending and continue only supported independent work. This condition also applies to the completion criteria below.

1. Read Settings and Language Policy. Use Planning to verify remaining availability after as_of. A person's FTE across roles and projects must not exceed their available proportion.
2. Proposed/ready work uses original estimates; in_progress/blocked work needs Remaining O/M/P. Done/cancelled work consumes no future capacity. Unscheduled unknowns remain explicit.
3. Run plan and inspect overload, dependencies, external waiting and deadlines. Person-days measure effort, not calendar duration; three-point expected effort is not a delivery probability.
4. Propose concrete splitting, scope, date or allocation options with their costs and conditions. Preserve the prior forecast and actual decision before recalculating.

Complete when scheduled work has estimates supported by supplied inputs or an explicit evidence-based method, and remaining effort and availability use the same time basis. Ask for missing estimation inputs and external commitments; keep dependent forecasts pending. Record the basis for team review and no unsupported delivery commitment.
