# Business requirements

Apply [Evidence and Clarification Policy](../Evidence-and-Clarification-Policy.md) whenever information is unclear, missing or conflicting. Ask the user for clarification or relevant material; keep dependent conclusions pending and continue only supported independent work. This condition also applies to the completion criteria below.

1. Read Settings and Language Policy. Use the [discovery procedure](../../00_Governance/Discovery-and-Prioritization.md) to inspect actors, triggers, current workflow, pain, cause, impact, success measures and constraints.
2. Separate proposed solutions from actual needs. System evidence reveals constraints; it does not create business goals. Ask about unsupported claims and request their evidence; keep them pending. Attribute stakeholder-supplied assumptions without adopting them as facts.
3. Write BRD goals with a baseline, measurement definition and owner; include scope, exclusions, business rules, stakeholders and conflicts.
4. Finish supported independent analysis and present every unresolved question or material request to the user. Ask for the owner and needed date when absent; leave them null until supplied. Draft generation is not approval.

Complete when problems and claims are traceable, goals are measurable or have a measurement plan, scope is clear and unknowns remain explicit. Run validate.
