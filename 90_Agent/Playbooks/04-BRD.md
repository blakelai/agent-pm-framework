# Business requirements

1. Read Settings and Language Policy. Use the [discovery procedure](../../00_Governance/Discovery-and-Prioritization.md) to inspect actors, triggers, current workflow, pain, cause, impact, success measures and constraints.
2. Separate proposed solutions from actual needs. System evidence reveals constraints; it does not create business goals. Mark unsupported claims as assumptions.
3. Write BRD goals with a baseline, measurement definition and owner; include scope, exclusions, business rules, stakeholders and conflicts.
4. Finish independent analysis and record critical open questions with an owner and needed date. Draft generation is not approval.

Complete when problems and claims are traceable, goals are measurable or have a measurement plan, scope is clear and unknowns remain explicit. Run validate.
