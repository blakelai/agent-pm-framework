# Discovery and prioritization

Identify sponsor, business/process owner, users, approvers, system/data owners and operational support. Inspect the current workflow with triggers, inputs, decisions, pain, workarounds, volumes, exceptions and constraints. Separate reported pain from inferred causes; preserve contradictory perspectives with evidence.

Define target outcomes, current baselines, measurement method, owner and observation window. Keep unknown metrics as pending with a measurement plan. Compare process simplification, configuration, manual controls and software changes before selecting a solution.

Order candidate outcomes by business impact, urgency, risk reduction, confidence, effort and dependencies. Document the actual PO/sponsor trade-off rather than treating a calculated score as authority. Record scope, exclusions, assumptions, hard constraints and decision owners. Refine the next valuable slice; revisit ordering when evidence changes.
