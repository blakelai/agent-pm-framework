# Outcome measurement

## Business outcome and measurement definition

Record the supplied baseline, target, metric, unit, data sources and observation window. Unknown values remain null; zero is an actual supplied value.

## Actual observation and external influences

Describe the actual observation, evidence, limitations and external influences. Measured does not mean the target was achieved or that the project caused the observed change.

## Cost and effort context

Link forecasts and actual costs when supplied, with currency, units and time basis. Request missing rates and cost information; do not invent them.

## Follow-up and learning

Link actual follow-up actions or backlog work. Preserve prior measurement records using previous_records.
