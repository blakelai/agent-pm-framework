# Benefits and cost review

For each business outcome, record baseline, target, measurement definition, data source, owner and observation window. Compare actual adoption and process outcomes with expectations; identify external influences and avoid attributing every change to the project.

Track forecast versus actual role effort, rate assumptions, infrastructure, licensing, vendor work, training and operations. Label unconfirmed rates or costs as assumptions; record cost units/currency and time basis. The framework does not connect to a finance system or automatically authorize expenditure.

Review realized benefits, ongoing support load, incidents and technical debt. Assign follow-up and prioritize subsequent work with business owners. Preserve prior forecasts and decisions so variance is explainable.
