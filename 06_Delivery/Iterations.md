# Iterations

Add agreed planning periods and goals. Dates are planning inputs; capacity overload does not automatically move a date. Review holidays, external dependencies and deployment windows separately.

| Sprint | Start | End | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|

Use integer Sprint identifiers and ISO dates (YYYY-MM-DD). Periods must not overlap. Empty periods mean the delivery plan is not configured.
